Why donate?

Responsible consumption doesn't end with what we buy. It also includes what we do with the things we no longer need. Every year, huge amounts of food, clothes and household items are thrown away while they could still help someone else. Donating is one of the simplest ways to reduce waste, give resources a second life and support your community at the same time.

Charities depend on the generosity of people like you to keep their work going. Your donations help them provide meals to families, warm clothes to people who need them and essential services to those facing difficult times. You don't need to give a lot to make a difference: every contribution counts. Explore the charities below and choose a cause that matters to you. Together, we can build a fairer and more sustainable future.
